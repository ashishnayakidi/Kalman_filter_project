ℹ️ About This Dashboard
Click to expand
📊 Key Features
  • ✅ Real-time Monitoring: Track SOC, SOH, and battery health metrics
  • ✅ Interactive Charts: Visualize trends over time with dynamic filtering
  • ✅ Health Score: Comprehensive 0-100 health score with breakdown
  • ✅ Prediction Metrics: RUL, degradation rates, and range loss estimates
  • ✅ Quick Insights: AI-powered insights at a glance
🔧 Advanced Features
  • ✅ Time Range Filtering: View data for 24h, 7d, 30d, 3m, or all time
  • ✅ Alert System: Automatic detection of critical battery issues
  • ✅ Trend Indicators: See if metrics are improving or degrading
  • ✅ Benchmark Comparison: Compare your battery to industry standards
  • ✅ AI Chat & Reports: Get answers and weekly health reports
📈 Data Sources

This dashboard uses processed battery data from:

  • EKF Timeseries: Extended Kalman Filter state estimates (SOC, SOH, voltage predictions)
  • Daily Summaries: Aggregated daily statistics (energy, temperature, efficiency)
  • Prediction Metrics: Computed health indicators (degradation, RUL, thermal stress)
